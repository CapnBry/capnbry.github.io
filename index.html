Widscan58

A 5.8 GHz FPV channel scanner made from an ESP32-C5.

Widscan58 uses the Wi-Fi radio inside the ESP32-C5 as a software-defined radio. Instead of decoding Wi-Fi, it reads raw I/Q samples straight from the radio and shows what is on each FPV video channel: signal strength, noise floor, SNR, video bandwidth and a live spectrum of the whole band. Use it at the field to find a clear channel or check pilot VTX frequencies.

  • Eight channels at once, each updating about 60 times a second
  • Band panorama with max-hold, average traces and a waterfall
  • RotorHazard timer node or a standalone lap timer with voice callouts
  • Flash from the browser, or press Simulate to try it with no hardware

FNB48 Web Control

A browser replacement for the FNIRSI FNB48 Android app.

The official app doesn't seem to work any more, so this one talks to the USB power meter directly over Web Bluetooth. The protocol was reverse-engineered by disassembling the APK. It installs on your phone as a PWA, and works in Chrome or Edge on desktop and Android.

  • Live V / A / W at 0.1 mV / 0.1 mA, plus temperature, D+/D− and charge protocol
  • Live trace from 10 s to 1 h with min/max/avg and freeze
  • Fast-charge trigger for QC2.0, QC3.0, FCP and AFC
  • Energy groups, alarms and recording to CSV
